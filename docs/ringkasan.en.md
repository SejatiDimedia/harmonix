# Harmonix: Self-Service Data Analytics Platform for Business Teams

An artificial intelligence data analytics assistant that translates everyday business questions into verified, secure data insights for decision makers.

## Background

Daily business decision-making often stalls due to long queues of ad-hoc report requests waiting on data teams. At the same time, granting non-technical teams direct database access or connecting generic commercial artificial intelligence models introduces substantial risks, ranging from unintentional data corruption and customer privacy leaks to fabricated figures that mislead strategic decisions.

## Project Goals

Deliver a self-service analytics platform that enables business stakeholders to obtain data insights, structured summary tables, and visual charts directly using natural language, backed by complete data security guarantees, numerical verification to eliminate calculation errors, and zero-cost infrastructure efficiency.

## Business Solutions

### Natural Language Data Consultation
Users can submit routine operational questions in plain language to receive structured summaries, data tables, and interactive charts without needing any database query skills.

### Absolute Operational Data Protection
The system enforces strict read-only access. Any command with the potential to modify, corrupt, or delete business records is automatically blocked before ever reaching the database server.

### Independent Cross-Checking Against Calculation Errors
Before an answer appears on screen, an independent digital auditor verifies the underlying data logic and matches every figure cited in the text narrative against the raw calculation tables, ensuring no fabricated numbers reach the user.

### Governance Approval Gate for Sensitive Requests
Queries involving unusually large data volumes or sensitive business parameters are paused automatically for review and approval by designated data managers before processing.

### Automated Masking of Sensitive Customer Data
Customer personal information such as email addresses and phone numbers is redacted automatically before results are presented, protecting customer privacy and supporting data governance compliance.

### Executive Dashboard Canvas and Fullscreen Display
Important findings and visual charts can be pinned to a unified dashboard with flexible layouts, complete with a dedicated fullscreen mode tailored for conference room displays and monitoring screens.

### Self-Service Data Health Auditing
Users can upload custom spreadsheet files to instantly review data completeness, detect statistical outliers, and receive a data health rating before beginning their analysis.

### Proactive Business Indicator Monitoring
The system monitors key operational metrics in the background on regular schedules, automatically dispatching early warnings and root-cause diagnoses whenever anomalies or performance drops occur.

## Results

Based on structured evaluation across dozens of analytical and safety benchmarks:
- 100% protection against database manipulation attempts and sensitive data exposure, with zero security breaches.
- 95.6% accuracy in tool selection for completing analytical requests.
- Execution accuracy reached 82.2% in isolated baseline testing and 91.1% in cloud model testing.
- 94.2% factual consistency score between narrative summaries and raw data figures.
- Zero dollar operational inference cost achieved through a hybrid setup pairing local execution models with free-tier cloud resources.

## Technology

Python, FastAPI, PostgreSQL with pgvector, Apache ECharts, React, TypeScript, and local or cloud language model integrations.
